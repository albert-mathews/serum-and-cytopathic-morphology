# Description
Collection of institutionbal guildelines with virus isolation/culturing protocols.
The intent here is to capture the protocol steps where innoculum is added, to see what other changes are are simultaneously made to the culture system.
Secondly, the end-point for viral presence is recorded. i.e. is presence of CPE stated as synonymous with viral presence.




# ATCC Virology Culture Guide
https://www.atcc.org/resources/culture-guides/virology-culture-guide

## insitution description
American Type Culture Collection
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATCC_(company)

## Inncoluation
- “Viral growth medium is usually supplemented with a lower percentage of serum than cell growth medium, often ranging between 2-10% depending on the virus” (p. 4)
- “NOTE 5: For viral inoculation, ATCC recommends decreasing the percentage of serum to 2% as it can interfere with viral attachment. This can vary from strain to strain, and is not applicable for influenza viruses.”
- Procedure: Remove cell growth medium, wash monolayer with PBS, inoculate with viral stock prepared in base medium (without serum) unless specified, then add/maintain in viral growth/maintenance medium.

## end points
see section “Viral authentication and viability testing”
- CPE (primary visual indicator; cells checked daily for rounding, refractility, degeneration, etc.)
- Hemagglutination, hemadsorption, sequencing, ELISA (supplemental).

## Discussion
the ATCC is not explicit about the protocol, it is hidden within the 'Notes'.

## Overview
FBS pre-innoculation: 2-10%
FBS post-innoculation: 2%
Is CPE a viral presence indicator: yes




# CLSI M41a
https://clsi.org/shop/standards/m41/

## insitution description
Clinical and Laboratory Standards Institute
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinical_and_Laboratory_Standards_Institute

## Innoculation
- section “5.3.1.2 Cell Density”: “Once the cultures have reached the required density, maintenance medium containing 2% fetal bovine serum (FBS) can be used in place of growth medium, which usually contains 10% FBS. Medium with the lower concentration of FBS will maintain cell viability and will slow monolayer overgrowth.”
- section “5.3.2 Maintenance”: “Refeed monolayers that are at 75% or greater confluence with fresh maintenance medium containing a low concentration of FBS (e.g., 2%) when the medium color indicates that the pH is below 7.0 (yellow-orange to yellow).”
- section “5.4.1 Medium Composition”: “Typically 10% FBS is used for growth medium intended for the propagation of cell culture monolayers, while a concentration of 1 to 3% is utilized to maintain subconfluent to confluent monolayers both before and after inoculation.”
- section “7.1.2 Preinoculation Assessment of Monolayers”: “Inspect cell culture microscopically and macroscopically as described in Section 5.3.1 just prior to specimen inoculation. In summary: the cell culture medium must not be cloudy or turbid; cells should be adherent and healthy in appearance; and the monolayer should be 75 to 90% confluent, but not overgrown.”
## end points
see section “7.4.2 Viral Effects”
- CPE (primary)
- hemagglutination (supplemental)

## Discussion
CLSI describes culture medium with 'cell manuafacturer recommend FBS concentration of FBS'(typically 10%) as 'growth medium'.
CLSI describes culture medium with 2% FBS concentration as 'maintenance medium'.
CLSI dictates that beyond 75% confluence, 'maintenance medium' shall be used.
CLSI dictates that confluencey should be 75-90% for innoculation.

Together, those 4 requirements above mean that in general, pre-innoculation FBS concentration would be typically 10%, while post-innoculation would be 2%.
The protocol does not explicitly state that FBS reduction is coincident with inoculation. Combining the listed constraints is consistent with a typical pattern of ~10% pre-inoculation and ~2% post-inoculation; actual lab practice may vary.

## Overview
FBS pre-innoculation: 10%
FBS post-innoculation: 2%
Is CPE a viral presence indicator: yes

FBS post-innoculation: 2%
Is CPE a viral presence indicator: yes




# NEADL - Virus isolation of peste des petits ruminants
https://collaboratif.cirad.fr/alfresco/s/d/workspace/SpacesStore/73d6ddd2-aaa4-4b4f-964e-b88ac7eff05b
Standard Operating Procedure (veterinary diagnostic lab, e.g., CIRAD/NEADL collaborative)

## insitution description
CIRAD (French Agricultural Research Centre for International Development)
https://www.cirad.fr/en

EURL-PPR (European Union Reference Laboratory for Peste des Petits Ruminants, hosted at CIRAD)
https://eurl-ppr.cirad.fr/

NEADL (National Exotic Animal Disease Laboratory)

## Innoculation
- Cells grown in growth media (DMEM with 10% FBS and 1% antibiotics) in 25 cm² flask.
- “Remove the growth media of Vero dog-slam cells. Wash the monolayer with sterile PBS one time.”
- Add 200 µl supernatant … Incubate 1 hour.
- “Add maintenance media (DMEM with 2% FBS and 1% antibiotics) to the final volume of 5ml.”

## end point
- “Cultures are examined for cytopathic effect (CPE). … Check every day for the appearance of cytopathic effects (CPE).”
- Results section: CPE (cell rounding, aggregation, syncytia) confirms PPRV; blind passages if needed.

## Discussion
Explicit, step-wise reduction of FBS exactly at inoculation time. CPE is the primary daily observation endpoint.

## Overview
FBS pre-inoculation: 10%
FBS post-inoculation: 2%
Is CPE a viral presence indicator: yes (primary; can be confirmed by neutralization/other).

# USDA APHIS Protocol (VIRPRO1013)
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/sites/default/files/VIRPRO1013.pdf
These protocols are intended for testing Master-Seed-Virus (MSV) used for vaccine production.
The goal is to neutralize the MSV, and the use cell culture and CPE to test for extraneous cytopathogenic agents.

## insitution description
United States Department of Agriculture - Animal and PLant Health Inspection Service
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/

## Innoculation
2.2.9 Growth Medium
	1. 900 mL of MEM
	2. Aseptically add 50-100 mL gamma-irradiated fetal bovine serum (FBS)
2.2.10 Maintenance Medium
	1. 900 mL of MEM
	2. Aseptically add 50-200 mL gamma-irradiated FBS

based on volumes, the growth medium is 5-10% FBS, and the maintenance medium is 5-20% FBS.

## end point




## insitution description

## Inncoluation

- "9.4.10 maintenance": "Cells should be passaged at least one time each week. Cells can be maintained for several days by switching to medium containing 2% FBS to prevent overgrowth."
- 9.4.11 Inoculation": "For inoculations, cells should be seeded into 25cm2  tissue culture flasks (T-25). Cells should be at approximately 85-90% of confluency and at least one day after seeding. ## end points
