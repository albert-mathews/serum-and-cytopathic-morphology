# VI230 — Summary (Canil, Pratt, Sungu, Phillips 1985)

**Full cite:** K. A. Canil, D. Pratt, M. S. Sungu, P. A. Phillips. "Influenza surveillance: alternative laboratory techniques for a developing country." *Bulletin of the World Health Organization* 63(1):79–82 (1985). Papua New Guinea Institute of Medical Research, Goroka.

## Owner's note (confirmed)

"They say they're developing a protocol, but they're doing virus isolation practically in order to evaluate lower-cost cells for the same purpose" — **correct.** This is a **practical surveillance program** demonstrating trypsin-treated LLC-MK2 + indirect immunofluorescence (IFAT) as a low-cost alternative to primary MK / embryonated eggs in a resource-limited setting. It describes **routine isolation of clinical specimens** (416 nasopharyngeal aspirates over the study), not a hypothetical protocol.

## What was done

- **Specimens:** 416 NPAs from children with acute respiratory illness (ARI) at Goroka Base Hospital, plus 15 throat swabs from a village outbreak of influenza-like illness.
- **Isolation cells:** HEp-2, human embryonic lung fibroblasts, and **LLC-MK2** (trypsin-treated).
- **Readout:** CPE (HEp-2/fibroblasts, 3×/week) + **hemadsorption (HAd)** with guinea-pig erythrocytes (LLC-MK2, 2×/week).
- **Identification:** indirect fluorescent antibody technique (IFAT) on CPE+/HAd+ cultures, plus IFAT directly on NPA pellet slides (8-antiserum panel). Influenza isolates serotyped at WHO Reference Centre (Melbourne).

## FBS manipulation (R1-relevant)

| Cell / stage | Medium |
|---|---|
| HEp-2, HEL fibroblast, LLC-MK2 **growth** | Eagle MEM + 2% HEPES + **5–10% FCS** |
| Same cells **maintenance** | Eagle MEM + **1–2% FCS** |
| LLC-MK2 **post-inoculation** | **Serum-free** Medium 199 + 6 µg/ml crystalline trypsin |
| Pre-inoculation LLC-MK2 | rinsed **3× with HBSS to remove serum** |

So the order is explicit: growth 5–10% FCS → maintenance 1–2% FCS → for LLC-MK2 (the myxovirus isolation line), **serum removed by washing, then serum-free trypsin medium** after inoculation. The serum is deliberately washed out before virus is added.

## Results

- 416 NPAs: Influenza A isolated in 17 (IFAT co-positive 77%); Influenza B in 3 (co-positive 75%). Overall correlation vs IFAT ~97–99%.
- 7/15 throat swabs from the outbreak yielded Influenza A (typed A/Philippines/2/82); influenza B isolates typed B/Singapore/222/79.
- Dual infections (influenza + RSV) found in 3 specimens.

## Why this matters to R1

1. A **developing-country, cost-driven** demonstration that the standard dual-media → serum-free maintenance step is followed in routine surveillance practice.
2. Explicit **serum-removal-by-wash before inoculation** — an unusually direct statement that serum is deliberately excluded from the inoculation/maintenance step for myxovirus isolation.
3. FBS pre 5–10%, post 1–2% (HEp-2/fibroblast) and 0% (LLC-MK2), consistent with the corpus's dual-media pattern.

## Category recommendation

**Keep in VI (category 1).** It is a genuine primary-isolation surveillance program; the "protocol development" framing is secondary to the actual isolation of clinical viruses. No re-categorization.
