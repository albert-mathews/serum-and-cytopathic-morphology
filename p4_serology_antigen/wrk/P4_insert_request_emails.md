**Method correction (2026-11-17, with Albert).** The inserts disclose only "recombinant VP7" (BTC)
and "purified IBV recombinant protein" (IBVARSV2) — no expression host, no ORF source strain. That
silence is **itself the finding** (non-disclosure in the manufacturer's own citable document), not a
question to chase by email: **a sales rep's reply is not citable**, so it cannot supply the provenance
the census needs. The provenance must be traced through the **published record** instead — the
antigen class's own expression/origin papers, then their source strain traced to culture (P3-style).
