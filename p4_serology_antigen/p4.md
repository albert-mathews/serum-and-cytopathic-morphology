**Status:** In progress. Census 21 rows; §B prior-art read & re-tagged; R1 corpus read in full (184 rows) and the serology-relevant subset coded confirmatory/challenging (§4h–4j) — `p4_r1_roster.csv`. Prediction broadened 2026-11-12 to a second observable (healthy/no-virus-expected sample).

## Prediction

If culture CPE supernatant is not specific to viral presence, and serology tests are designed
with that supernatant as their reference (oracle), then the published record should contain cases
where **serology tests read positive on cell-culture reagents of the kind found in that
supernatant** — without requiring a virus to explain the signal.

*(Short form: serology tuned on CPE supernatant will react to culture-reagent material, not only
to a virus.)*

Full motivation, tests, strengthen/weaken and non-goals: `P4_prediction.md`.

This hub will hold coding sheets, corpora, and snippets when the stream starts.

Claims in this stream are limited to what the cited corpus documents about Isolation-linked practice and inheritance risk.

## Snippets (paper-ready prose)

**P4-S1 — there is no "perfect mock" (a matched uninfected culture cannot establish epitope identity).**
A matched uninfected culture is not a "no-virus" control in the sense the conclusion needs, because
there is no process that yields a pure viral-particle preparation to subtract. The infected and
uninfected cultures therefore differ by far more than the presence or absence of virus: the infected
culture is a degenerating monolayer whose cell debris, released host proteins, membrane vesicles, and
altered medium have no counterpart in the healthy mock. A positive signal on the infected culture that
is absent from the mock consequently shows only that the signal segregates with the infected state —
not that the reacting molecule is a viral epitope.

**P4-S2 — the recombinant antigen retraces, not escapes, the culture anchor.**
A recombinant antigen leaves the culture only at the production step, not at the point where it is
defined. Because its reading frame is in practice cloned from a reference strain that was itself
declared a culture+CPE Isolate — and whose assembly from a mixed culture product is, per P3, far from
error-free — the recombinant protein is the culture-direct antigen with a translation step interposed,
not an independent measurement of it.

### Coding rules (P4-specific)

- A ref is **confirmatory** if the antibody/antigen binds the culture product (infected monolayer,
  culture lysate, culture supernatant, or a live culture-propagated virus read by VN/CPE/HA/HI).
- A ref is **recombinant/partial** if it binds a defined recombinant protein whose ORF-source isolate is
  not stated in-paper (per §4f·i, recombinance does not by itself escape the culture anchor).
- A ref is **challenging** if the antigen is genuinely not culture-routed (clinical tissue section, or a
  kit whose antigen is not disclosed at all).
- Quotes are verbatim and tagged `[VERIFIED src]` (full text) — abstract-derived claims are excluded.

## Discussion

The prediction runs on two halves, which now carry **unequal weight** after full-text reading.

**Provenance half — robust.** Across the kit census (21 rows), the §B prior-art set, and the R1 corpus,
the reference material of serology/antigen tests is overwhelmingly the culture product itself. No
sampled sold test scores `independent`; every recombinant kit keys its antigen to a culture-seeded
"strain designation" whose ORF/CPE origin is unstated (§4g, P4-S2).

**Reactive-on-non-viral-reagent half — thinner, and properly qualified.** The strongest reference-side
case is Kainulainen 2026 (a "neutralizing" IgM that binds host GM2 ganglioside, virus-unexposed donors).
The patient-side interference literature (Song 2025 ~50% of HSV IgM positives are heterophile; Kricka;
Costa) is the same non-viral-epitope arm at a *different location*. But the two inference gaps in §4f
(the recombinant hatch, and the impossibility of a "perfect mock") bound how far any single positive can
be read as virus-specific: a nonreactive matched mock rules out only *baseline uninfected* reactivity,
not host/EV/serum material co-arising with infection.

**R1-specific finding (§4h–4j).** Of the R1 serology-relevant refs read in full: **12 confirmatory
(culture-routed), 1 recombinant-partial (VI205 gD), 2 genuinely-challenging (VI104 clinical-tissue IF;
VI97 commercial-kit ELISA with undisclosed antigen)**. Two endpoint flags (VI71, VI232) did not survive
full text — they are molecular/antisense papers mis-flagged as serology. This is the truth-not-bias
ledger: the near-total culture-routed pattern holds, but it is bounded by exactly the two classes of
non-culture route P4 predicted (clinical specimen; undisclosed reagent) and by two flagged data errors.

## Conclusion (relative to the P4 prediction)

The prediction's **provenance arm is supported**: serology/antigen reference material demonstrably
inherits the culture/FBS/CPE under-determination — no sampled kit or R1 paper grounds its antigen in a
demonstrated non-culture identity, and "recombinant" re-opens rather than closes the P3 link. The
prediction's **reactivity arm is supported but narrower than a strong reading would claim**: the
culture-reagent-positive case rests on one decisive reference-side anchor plus the patient-side
interference literature, and is qualified by the no-perfect-mock argument. P4's defensible claim is
therefore: *under standard Isolation practice, serology and antigen tests are tuned on culture-derived
material, so their agreement with Isolation labels is circular rather than triangulating — and no
examined test escapes that circle by an independent (non-culture) antigen.* What P4 does **not** claim:
that the culture-reagent-positive signal is demonstrated in every kit, or that serology's false-positive
literature is itself the culture-reagent mechanism. Remaining open: the deliberate counterexample hunt
(a sold test with a genuine clinical-direct/synthetic-peptide positive control) and the bioRxiv/medRxiv
preprint sweep (TODO 5).

**Prediction refinement (2026-11-12, with Albert).** The prediction now names **two observables**, both
non-viral-epitope, differing only by location: (1) a serology test positive on **culture-reagent
material** (reference-side; the P4-specific claim); (2) a serology test positive on a **healthy /
no-virus-expected patient sample** (patient-side; the field's own large false-positive/cross-reactivity
literature). Both are documented; (1) shows no-virus-*needed* directly, (2) shows a non-viral epitope
drives routine positives at population scale but is often explained as "a prior real virus," so it
enlarges without by itself settling the reference-side question. New OA leads (Grimm 2024 pre-pandemic
blood donors, Luo 2025 heterophile→syphilis, Nowak 2025 cross-reactivity review, Chen 2026
SARS-CoV-2→HIV false-positive rate) are on `P4_pdf_wishlist.md` P4W-05…P4W-08 and `P4_bibliography.md`
P4B-25…P4B-28, `[DATA REQUIRED]` until fetched and read in full.
