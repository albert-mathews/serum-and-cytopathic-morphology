# VIRPRO1013 analogs — cross-jurisdiction comparison

**Anchor (US):** USDA APHIS CVB **VIRPRO1013** / SAM312 — vaccine master-seed (MSV)
extraneous cytopathogenic-agent testing. Neutralize the MSV; use cell culture + CPE to
look for extraneous agents.
- Growth medium: **~5–10% FBS** (900 mL MEM + 50–100 mL FBS)
- Maintenance medium: **~5–20% FBS** (900 mL MEM + 50–200 mL FBS) → *hold or increase*
- Endpoint: CPE + hemadsorption + IF/other for extraneous/cytopathogenic agents

**Question being tested:** does the wide/high maintenance-serum pattern (VIRPRO's
5–20%, which can hold or exceed growth) recur in extraneous-agent / master-seed SOPs
in other jurisdictions, or is it US-specific?

## Headline finding (so far)

Across the EU (Ph. Eur.), EMA/CVMP, VICH, and WOAH extraneous-agent / master-seed
chapters, **serum percentage is NOT prescribed at all** — the chapters are
*performance/test-specification* based: they mandate replicate counts, monolayer surface
areas, survival-rate minima, negative/positive controls, and readouts (CPE, haemadsorption,
immunofluorescence, NAT), while leaving growth vs maintenance medium composition to the
manufacturer ("same medium and additives... as those used for the preparation of the
vaccine"). The only serum constraint is **antibody-freedom / inhibitory-freedom** (e.g.
Ph. Eur. 2.6.24 §7; WOAH 1.1.9 serum-freedom clauses), i.e. serum *quality*, not *quantity*.

This contrasts with the US VIRPRO1013, which is *recipe-prescriptive* — it states serum
**volumes** (5–10% growth; 5–20% maintenance), producing the hold-or-increase maintenance
pattern that motivated this inquiry.

**Implication:** the "wide/high maintenance serum" observation is a *feature of the US
volumetric-recipe style*, not a universal property of extraneous-agent SOPs. It forms a
useful contrast pair: US = volume-prescribed (and thereby testable for the FBS question);
EU/WOAH/VICH = volume-unspecified (so the FBS question is answered at the manufacturer
level — which is exactly the gap the US FOIA was designed to probe for the US side).

## Caveats / next steps

- Ph. Eur. text extracted is EP 7.0 (2010-2011). Confirm current edition (Ph. Eur. 11.x)
  retains the volume-unspecified stance (the "new approach" 2020 change was about
  harmonizing EA management, not serum %).
- WOAH Terrestrial Manual chapter 1.1.9 fetched in full (16 pp) — volume-unspecified
  confirmed; it even *cites 9 CFR* (US) as a reference for the serum-freedom tests.
- The positive finding here is an *absence* (no serum % specified) across EU/WOAH/VICH;
  that must be stated as such, not over-read.
- Still open: confirm EMA public assessment reports / national authorities (VMD, ANSES,
  APVMA, Health Canada) also defer serum % to the manufacturer dossier. This is the
  "how do we find 'the medium used to prepare the vaccine'?" question: the number lives in
  the marketing-authorization dossier, retrievable only via public assessment reports or
  FOI/access-to-documents — not in the pharmacopoeial/manual text.
