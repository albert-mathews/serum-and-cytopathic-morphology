# VIRPRO1013 exclusion note

**Date:** 2026-09-18 (ET)  
**Document:** USDA APHIS CVB **VIRPRO1013** — Master Seed Virus (MSV) / extraneous cytopathogenic agent testing protocol  
**URL:** https://www.aphis.usda.gov/sites/default/files/VIRPRO1013.pdf  
**Prior adversarial ID:** ADV011

## User rule (binding)

**VIRPRO1013 is NOT virus isolation / primary isolation / diagnostic culture from clinical or field specimens.**

It is a **vaccine master-seed purity** procedure: neutralize the MSV and use cell culture + CPE observation to look for **extraneous** agents. Do **not** merge it into the isolation-practice evidence set, even though its serum recipe can show post ≥ pre (growth medium ~5–10% FBS; maintenance medium ~5–20% FBS; inoculation step adds maintenance with 5–20% FBS).

## Why it looks tempting for X≤Y adversarial search

- Explicit dual volumes allow ~**5–10% → 5–20%** (hold or increase), which is the opposite of classic diagnostic 10→2 reduction.
- That contrast is scientifically interesting for incentive/CPE hypotheses, but **category error** if counted as isolation practice.

## Handling

- Tag in candidate tables: `EXCLUDED_not_isolation`
- Cite only in institutional/guideline side notes when discussing how safety/purity SOPs diverge from diagnostic isolation SOPs
- **Do not** use VIRPRO1013 as a HOLD / primary-isolation counterexample
