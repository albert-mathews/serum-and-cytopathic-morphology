# R4 - Non-isolation cell-culture standards and practice

## Purpose
R1 found that isolation protocols and isolation papers use two serum levels: growth medium with higher FBS, and a reduced-FBS medium at inoculation. R4 asks whether the same dual-serum pattern also appears in cell-culture work that is not virus isolation.

## Research questions
1. Do non-isolation cell-culture protocols (standards, guidelines, regulatory procedures) prescribe a reduced-serum step, and if so, at what stage, at what percentages, and with what stated rationale?
2. When work is carried out under those protocols, what do the published papers and reports show was actually done (serum levels, readouts, controls)?

## Search structure (mirrors R1)
- (c) **Non-isolation protocols:** cell-culture protocols outside virus isolation, such as master-seed and extraneous-agent testing, cell-bank qualification, toxicity or cytotoxicity assays, and routine maintenance and passage guides.
- (d) **Non-isolation practice:** papers and reports that document work done under the protocols found in (c).

## Seed items
- USDA APHIS VIRPRO1013 (master-seed extraneous-agent testing). This came from R1, where it was kept in the table but excluded from the isolation plots.

## Data to extract per item
For each protocol or paper: source, year, purpose of the culture, cell line, growth FBS %, maintenance or inoculation FBS %, the step where serum changes, stated rationale (verbatim), readout (for example CPE or morphology), and whether a negative control is described.
