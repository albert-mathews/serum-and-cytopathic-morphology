## Glossary (used across this stream)

- **Ring test / ring trial** — one homogeneous panel (or a named stock) circulated to N labs, each running its
  own method, results compared to a reference or to the other labs.
- **EQA (External Quality Assessment)** — a recurring, organizer-run scheme that ships characterized specimens to
  participating labs and scores each against an assigned reference target; commonly used for proficiency/
  accreditation, incl. WHO network panels for virus *isolation, identification, and typing* in cell culture.

   only. This answers the prediction's "minimum n?" → **n ≥ 10 for a rate; descriptive below**.

## Snippets (paper-ready prose)

- **P7-S1** — Inter-lab EQA for cell-culture Isolation reports sensitivity spread concentrated at low viral
  titre. In the first WHO National Influenza Centre EQA (n=21), the lowest-correct samples were isolated by
  only 52–62% of laboratories, while high-titre samples were isolated by ≥90%; and 2 of 21 laboratories
  reported a positive Isolation result on a virus-free negative sample (P7-003) [VERIFIED src].
- **P7-S2** — Control practice varies across the same EQA panel. In that panel, a negative Isolation control
  was used by only 16 of 21 laboratories and a positive control by 12 of 21; CPE was the first growth readout
  for 19 of 21 (P7-003) [VERIFIED src].
- **P7-S3** — The Isolation endpoint is the least reproducible of the methods compared. In a 40-laboratory
  BVDV proficiency trial, virus isolation in cell culture reached only 43.33% sensitivity (95% CI
  30.59–56.76%) while the antigen ELISA and RT-qPCR were reliable; the authors call isolation "time-consuming
  and insensitive" and unsuitable for routine eradication testing (P7-001) [VERIFIED src].
- **P7-S4** — Inter-lab concordance in cell-culture Isolation rises after process harmonization. In the WHO
  National Influenza Centre panels, the fraction of laboratories scoring 15/15 correct Isolation rose from
  6/19 (2016) to 11/19 (2019) (p=0.01), an improvement the authors attribute to follow-up, in-country
  training, and corrective measures rather than to any change in serum or reading threshold (P7-002, P7-003)
  [VERIFIED src].
- **P7-S5** — Assay-to-assay spread dwarfs lab-to-lab spread within one ring trial. Across 7 virus-isolation
  assays run on identical swine oral-fluid samples, the detection-dose-50 spanned a 102.67-fold range
  (10⁻¹·⁴¹ to 10⁻⁴·⁰⁸), and the authors report "a general lack of reproducibility among the participating
  laboratories" (P7-004) [VERIFIED src].
- **P7-S6** — High concordance is achievable, but under a shared template. A standardized anti-AAV9
  microneutralization assay reached 23–46% GCV across three laboratories only after the method had been
  standardized, transferred, and its key materials supplied; the authors state accuracy was "achieved by
  strictly implementing SOPs, standardizing key materials, QC criteria and modeling the IC50 endpoint"
  (P7-006) [VERIFIED src]. This is the near-side counterexample: concordance tracks the template, not
  independent discovery.
- **P7-S7** — Inter-lab discordance in serology is driven by kit and incubation protocol rather than by
  independent replication of one assay. In a 51-laboratory BVDV antibody ring trial, four pooled antibody-
  positive milk samples were called negative in 43.6%, 50.9%, 3.6% and 56.4% of investigations respectively,
  with the misses tracking ELISA-kit choice and short-versus-overnight incubation (P7-007) [VERIFIED src].
- **P7-S8** — The Isolation/titration endpoint is less reproducible than molecular detection even within a
  single shared panel. In a three-reference-laboratory African swine fever ring trial, the probability of
  detecting infectious virus in cells ranged from 0.68 to 0.90 across six infectivity assays (highest with
  primary alveolar macrophages, lowest with a wild-boar lung cell line), while qPCR detected viral DNA with
  probability 0.96–1.0; the authors attribute the infectivity spread to cell sensitivity, cell density,
  inoculum, incubation, staining, and operator experience in manual microscope reading (P7-008) [VERIFIED src].
- **P7-S9** — Serology discordance recurs as kit/protocol drift, not lab drift. In a 39-participant German
  BVDV ring trial the antigen/genome panel was reliable (antigen ELISA 100%, RT-PCR 98.73%), but four of nine
  antibody ELISA kits needed adjustment and two antibody-positive milk samples were called false-negative in
  up to six cases, tracking short-versus-overnight incubation (P7-009) [VERIFIED src].
- **P7-S10** — What "concordance" means depends on the endpoint. In a 15-laboratory SARS-CoV-2 antigenicity
  comparison using common reagents, relative fold-drops were consistent (RMSD 0.59), but absolute titre
  magnitude varied by ~3.2 log2 (~9-fold) between live-virus and pseudovirus neutralization even within the
  same assay group (P7-010) [VERIFIED src].
- **P7-S11** — Serology discordance can be endpoint-specific within one panel. In a 16-laboratory
  classical/African swine fever proficiency exercise, molecular detection was near-perfect (ASF 100%, CSF
  85.7%) and ASF serology 80%, but CSF serology was only 35.7% accurate (P7-011) [VERIFIED src].
- **P7-S12** — The human-microscopy readout underperforms the machine readout even where no cell-culture
  propagation is involved. In a 13-lab Sub-Saharan African rabies trial the direct fluorescent-antibody test
  (read by microscope) reached 87.69% concordance (sensitivity 89.23%, specificity 86.15%) while RT-PCR reached
  98.2% (96.97%, 100%) (P7-012) [VERIFIED src]. This isolates the *visual-judgment* component of the C080
  asymmetry from the *cell-culture propagation* component — the same human-vs-machine contrast that also
  characterizes CPE reading.

## C057 assessment (against the 11-row corpus)

**Claim under test (C057, Conjecture):** *Cross-lab replication reproduces the shared culture process, not
an observationally unique particle; inter-lab agreement is therefore weak evidence of particle identity on
its own.*

The prediction's test is whether Isolation-linked endpoints show **template-following concordance** (agreement
that tracks a shared recipe) rather than **robustness under deliberate serum/matrix/control variation**. The
11 rows are mapped below.

### Reading

1. **The Isolation/titration endpoint is consistently the least reproducible of the methods compared**
   (P7-001, P7-003, P7-004, P7-008). This is the empirical core: the endpoint that declares "virus present"
   is the one that varies most across laboratories, while molecular detection is near-perfect in the same
   panels. This is the pattern C057 predicts.

2. **Concordance, where high, tracks a shared template.** The before/after harmonization pair (P7-002) shows
   agreement rising only after follow-up, training, and corrective measures — not after any independent
   discovery. The transferred-method case (P7-006) and the identical-supplied-template case (P7-005) show the
   same: high agreement is produced by sharing the recipe, not by converging on an independently grounded
   object.

3. **The one counterexample is near-side.** P7-006 shows high concordance (23–46% GCV) is achievable — but
   only after the method was standardized, transferred, and its key materials supplied. This is exactly the
   template-following concordance C057 predicts, not a refutation of it. No row shows an Isolation-linked
   endpoint that is *stable under deliberately varied serum/matrix with strong matched controls* — the
   "weaken" condition is untested in this corpus.

4. **"Concordance" is endpoint-dependent (P7-010).** Relative fold-drops are reproducible even when absolute
   titres are not. This refines C057: the claim bears on the *absolute* endpoint (titre, CPE, particle
   identity), not on relative measures such as antigenic distance.

### Verdict

**C057 is supported by the corpus, not yet established.** All eleven rows are consistent with the
process-template reading; none contradicts it. But the corpus is *descriptive* (EQA/ring-trial reports), not a
controlled experiment: no row deliberately varies serum or matrix and shows the endpoint is stable under
Tier-A matched controls. The prediction's "weaken" condition therefore remains untested, and C057 stays a
Conjecture (supported by C027) rather than being upgraded to an Observation/Lemma.

### What would move C057

- **Upgrade toward a Lemma** if a controlled study varies serum/matrix with Tier-A matched controls and the
  Isolation endpoint is *stable* (the "weaken" condition) — that would bound the claim.
- **Strengthen further** if a study shows the endpoint *shifts* with serum lot without an inoculum change
  (failure mode 2), or if blinded raters disagree on CPE for identical images (failure mode 3 / R3).
- **Refine** with a same-stock assembly discordance study (P3 A3) to extend the claim to the sequence layer.

## Interpretive note — how C080 threads through R1–R3

The "Isolation/titration is least reproducible" finding (C080) is the fourth angle on an endpoint the rest of
the paper has already described three other ways. The through-line:

> The endpoint that declares "virus present" is (a) triggered by a non-inoculum cause (serum step-down, R3 /
> C040), (b) normally read without the control that would reveal that (R2 / C031), and (c) even in the
> best-case ring-trial setting, the noisiest readout of all the methods applied to the same sample (P7 / C080).

Each stream contributes one of the three facts, and together they describe a single property:

- **R1 (C020/C030)** — *what the shared template is*: the codified dual-serum step-down, ~10% FBS → ~2% FBS.
  This is the process every lab runs.
- **R2 (C023/C031)** — *that the audit control is missing*: matched-maintenance mocks are rare (~11/173 Tier A),
  so the CPE readout is normally made without the control that separates "serum did this" from "inoculum did
  this."
- **R3 (C040/C041)** — *that the confound is real and sufficient*: 10%→2% FBS alone, no virus, produces
  CPE-classified morphology; blinded raters score *more* CPE on the low-serum cultures.
- **P7 (C080)** — *that the endpoint is also the least reproducible*: even handed known-positive material,
  the cell-culture readout is the least sensitive and most variable of the methods compared.

The connection to C057 is then close to forced: the "reproducibility" that Isolation practice achieves is
**template-following**. Labs sharing the 10%→2% recipe produce the same confounded morphology and call it the
same thing ("CPE"), so they agree — but the agreement is on a shared artifact with a shared cause, not on
converging independently onto a specific particle.

**Scoping guard (kept explicit):** this is not a claim that the labs are incompetent — the ring trials prove
they are not (they hit 96–100% on molecular detection in the same panels). It is a claim that the *cell-culture
endpoint specifically* is the structural weak link, and that its irreproducibility is a property of the endpoint
(living-cell variables: cell line, passage, serum, confluency, incubation, manual microscope reading), not of
the laboratories.
