## Snippets (paper-ready prose)

**Purpose:** Carefully worded sentences/paragraphs for later drafting. Prefer these over improvised strong language.  
**Rules:** Process critique, not slogans. No “they never thought about medium.” Prefer deconfounding language. Update when wording is refined; do not delete superseded versions without marking them superseded.

### P1-S1 — Why medium rationales look virus-side (endpoint lock-in)

*Source: session 2026-07-22; agreed formulation for Discussion.*

> Once CPE under Isolation conditions is taken as the operational marker of virus, any medium effect that changes CPE readability will tend to be explained as an effect on the virus (or on “sensitivity” to virus). The mid-century record shows exactly that class of motives—antibodies, inhibitors, sensitivity—while lacking a foundational uninfected, medium-matched control tradition. That pattern is what one expects if **enrichment change at inoculation was never treated as a rival explanation of the CPE endpoint**. It does not prove that no infectious agent exists; it shows that the **inference CPE → virus was insulated from medium as a confounded co-variable**.

**Use with:** dual-medium primaries (P1-046, P1-047, P1-048, P1-206, P1-008); R2 Tier A rarity; CRO serum-only morphology.  
**Avoid pairing with:** “medium is the only real cause of CPE”; “foundational workers ignored serum entirely.”

### P1-S2 — “Serum-free” terminology (1960s vs modern SFM)

*Source: session 2026-07-22 terminology check.*

> In mid-century virus tissue-culture writing, “serum-free medium” usually meant **culture fluid to which no animal serum was added** (often a basal/synthetic mixture used for maintenance or assay after monolayers had been grown with serum). That is **not** synonymous with later commercial **serum-free media (SFM)** formulated as complete systems for **serum-independent cell growth**. In this paper, unless quoting, we use “**no serum added**” or “**serum omitted**” for the 1950s–60s operational sense.

### P1-S3 — Defendable vs too-strong history claims (checklist)

| Defendable | Too strong |
|------------|------------|
| Enrichment change is systematically co-varied with inoculation under Isolation protocols | Medium is “the” variable most correlated with CPE in nature |
| Historical rationales treat medium as modulator of virus CPE, not as alternative cause of the endpoint | They “never looked back” / “overlooked medium entirely” |
| No found foundational serum-only IV series on matched uninfected monolayers inventing Isolation CPE specificity | Absence of that paper is a literature gap, not an ontological proof |
| Dual media operational by ~1953–54; codified ~1969 (often ~2% FBS) | Continuous smooth evolution of FBS % from 1900 |
