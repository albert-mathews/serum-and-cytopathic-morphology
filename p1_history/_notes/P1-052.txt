=== KEY VERBATIM PASSAGES (English body + Summary; light OCR cleanup of O/o→%) ===

Growth / switch:
"Primary dog kidney (DK) cells were grown as monolayers in glass test tubes
under the medium with 10% bovine serum. Cell sheets were washed twice and then
rolled at 37C in a serum-free maintenance medium which contained given
ingredients under study."

Reference standard:
"Cultures in Eagle's minimum essential medium (MEM) with 5% calf serum were
regarded as the standard of reference."

Serum-free maintenance composition:
"Eagle's MEM containing 0.1% yeast extract (powdered, Difco), 0.1% skim milk
(powdered, Difco), 0.35% brain heart infusion broth (powdered, Difco), 0.15%
phytone (BBL), 0.04% polyvinylpyrrolidone (PVP, K-90, Cica); and 0.5% raw DK
cell extract fluid was then added. In this medium the cell sheet could be kept
in good condition for about 7 to 10 days without renewal of the medium."

Problem with serum (inhibitor lineage):
"This need causes problems in research on viruses, such as, endogenous
contaminants of viruses and mycoplasmas (4), impossibility to heat over 56C,
inhibitors and antibodies to viruses, lack of uniformity in quality, necessity
of freeze-storage, high cost etc."

Titration + field protocol (controls named):
"Parallel infectivity titrations of some canine viruses, including adeno-,
herpes-, parainfluenza-, and reovirus, were carried out with DK cell cultures.
The cultures containing the substitute mixture described above presented
infectivity titers almost the same as, or higher than, the cultures containing
serum."

"Four passages were made of inoculated and uninoculated control cell cultures
before the purification of transmissible CPE-producing agents by the successive
limiting dilution method."

Field result:
"A total of 151 strains of CPE-producing agents were isolated from 114 dogs
derived from various environments (Table 2) and their identification is now in
progress at this laboratory. The strains tested to date were not sensitive to
antibiotics against mycoplasma..."

Summary counts:
"With this medium a total of 151 strains of transmissible CPE-producing agents
were isolated from 537 specimens, including rectal, nasal, and pharyngeal
swabs, collected from 265 apparently healthy dogs."

Self-caveats:
"A possible cause for the unusually high frequency of isolation of viruses may
be that the medium is devoid of antibodies and serum inhibitors. Some of the
viruses recovered may reflect contamination of cell cultures."

"Comparison of virus isolations between the media with and without serum is
left for a future study."
