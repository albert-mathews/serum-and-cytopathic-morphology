- **Prediction (CNJ055 — "structure pipelines inherit the label").** Cryo-EM / tomography / "virus structure"
  pipelines that image particles purified from **culture + CPE Isolates** (or Isolation-maintenance supernatant)
  describe a *prepared particle ensemble*, not proof that Isolation's CPE endpoint was virus-specific (`CRL041`,
  `CRL042`) nor that the imaged class separates cleanly from co-purifying EVs (`CNJ052` → P2). High resolution and
  symmetry "beautifully describe a culture-product particle class" while leaving the Isolation warrant untouched.
  Licensed by the hinge `CNJ050` → `CNJ055`; premises `OB020` (modal 10%→2%) + `TH040` (serum step-down drives CPE
  morphology).
- **Methods (planned, no wet work).** A structure-paper worksheet codes each landmark map on an **upstream-warrant
  stack** (7 fields): (1) particle source — Isolate / supernatant / clinical fluid; (2) upstream Isolation endpoint —
  CPE / plaque / focus / PCR-only / unspecified; (3) culture conditions + growth→maintenance serum if stated (R1);
  (4) purification method + any EV-marker discussion (P2); (5) uninfected matched purification imaged? yes / no /
  unclear; (6) reconstruction particle selection — what was discarded as damaged/contaminant; (7) functional claim
  attached to the map — infectivity (= culture titer?). Sampling: ICTV exemplar / textbook structures; recent
  high-impact cryo-ET of "infected" cells (score CPE/serum disclosure); EV cryo-EM papers as a purification-language
  contrast class.
- **Results.** **Not started** — no corpus, no coding sheet, no snippets yet. The one-sentence prediction and the

- **Adversarial balance.** Weaken conditions defined up front: clinical-fluid particles with class-separating markers
  **plus** a non-culture functional assay; deliberate matched uninfected imaging showing *absence* of the reconstructed
  class. Non-goals: not anti-cryo-EM; maps are not claimed fabricated; scoped to **object selection and Isolation
  upstream**. Reconstruction quality **≠** warrant quality — a correct asymmetric unit can sit atop a culture+CPE
  object-selection step that R1–R2 show is recipe-heavy and often weakly controlled.
- **Conclusion.** `CNJ055` is **predicted, not started, and not established.** The empirical target is the *rate* at
  which structure pipelines disclose matched uninfected controls and EV-overlap tests — not whether Fourier shell
