\section*{Results}

\subsection*{Isolation methods pair inoculation with a serum step-down}
In the plotted dual-FBS corpus ($n=169$ of 170 rows parsed), pre-inoculation FBS had a mean of 9.30\% and median 10.0\%; post-inoculation FBS had a mean of 2.24\% and median 2.0\% (\texttt{expansion/fbs\_distribution\_plots/fbs\_distribution\_summary.txt}). Pre-inoculation values were 10\% in 139 of 169 papers; post-inoculation values were 2\% in 113 of 169, with a tail including 0\%, 1\%, 5\% and 10\%. The eight institutional recipes cluster at the same split (about 10\% growth to about 2\% maintenance; one measles recipe at 7.5\% to 2\%) (Fig~1). These frequencies describe practice as sampled and do not rank recipes.

\subsection*{Matched-medium negative controls are rarely reported}
Of 173 scored Isolation papers, 50 (28.9\%) reported an Isolation-stage negative control (Tiers A+B). Only 11 (6.4\%) reported one handled in the matched maintenance medium (Tier A); 38 reported an Isolation-stage control without a stated medium match (Tier B), 14 reported controls only at another stage (Tier C) and 110 (63.6\%) reported no qualifying control (Tier D) (\texttt{r2\_negative\_controls/r2.md}, status 2026-09-22). Absence or mismatch of controls is a methods-reporting finding; it does not by itself establish that CPE is non-specific in any particular laboratory system. \pending{Seven Tier-D rows await full text (\texttt{R2\_pdf\_wishlist.md}); tier counts may shift slightly.}

\subsection*{Lowering FBS alone produces CPE-classified morphology in uninoculated Vero E6 cells}
\emph{Contract laboratory descriptions.} On the 22 frames with their own CRO description, CPE-type terms were named on 1 of 9 Culture A (10\% FBS) frames and 10 of 13 Culture B (2\% FBS) frames. In Culture B the terms were rounded (8 frames), dying cells (7), granularity (6), vacuolation (4), detached (2) and refractile (1); the single Culture A mention was vacuolation. The mean share of the six CPE-type terms per frame was 0.02 in Culture A and 0.36 in Culture B (\texttt{cro-results/cro\_labels\_consolidation.md}).

\emph{Blinded raters, structured checklist (Pass 2).} Both raters scored more CPE-type morphology on Culture B. The mean CPE-item score sum (maximum 7) was 0.87 (A) vs 1.65 (B) for IR1 and 2.41 (A) vs 4.91 (B) for IR2. Items with the largest B$-$A incidence gaps were ballooned/enlarged (IR1 +0.62; IR2 +0.78) and cytoplasmic strands (IR1 +0.82); for IR2 also nonspecific degeneration (+1.00), syncytia (+0.55) and cell death (+0.22). IR2 rated 20 of 49 Culture B frames as not healthy overall and 29 as partially healthy, against 50 of 50 Culture A frames healthy; IR1 rated all frames healthy overall. IR2's Culture B CPE score rose monotonically from day 1 (3.48) to day 5 (6.34) with Culture A flat; IR1 showed a constant B$>$A offset across days (\texttt{ir-results/IR1\_IR2\_pass2\_side\_by\_side.md}).

\emph{Blinded raters, free text (Pass 1).} With the shared lexicon, IR2 named a CPE-type term on 19 of 50 Culture A and 25 of 49 Culture B frames; IR1's free text named rounded and refractile cells on every frame of both cultures, so its Pass-1 contrast is saturated (\texttt{IR1\_IR2\_pass1\_side\_by\_side.md}).

\emph{Briefing-condition pilot arm (IR3v).} \prov{IR3v Pass 1 covers all 100 frames (99 unique frames analysed). Under the clarified coding, a term from the CRO six was named on 36 of 50 Culture A and 40 of 49 Culture B frames: vacuolation 31 vs 30, rounded/shrinking 8 vs 12, refractile 17 vs 21, detached 0 vs 2 (shared lexicon, any CPE term: 38 vs 42). The single-large-vacuole observation, coded separately, was more frequent in Culture B (14 vs 24 frames), as were uncoded mentions of projections (10 vs 32). Culture B led on days 1--2 (7 and 8 of 10 frames vs 2 and 4) and both cultures reached 8--10 frames per day from day 3. On the 22 CRO-described frames IR3v named a CRO-six term on 5 of 9 (A) and 8 of 13 (B). Direction matches the other sources (B $\geq$ A); the gap (+10 percentage points) is similar to IR2 Pass 1 and smaller than for the CRO. \subsection*{Inter-rater agreement}
Raters agreed on direction (B $\geq$ A on aggregate CPE scores) but not on which features carry the difference: IR1 used vacuolation on no frame while IR2 marked it on every frame; IR2 marked syncytia and nonspecific degeneration on Culture B while IR1 marked neither (\texttt{IR1\_IR2\_pass2\_item\_agreement.csv}).
