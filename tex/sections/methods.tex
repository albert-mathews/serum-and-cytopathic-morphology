\section*{Materials and methods}

\subsection*{Search structure}
Literature and records were searched in four parts: (a) Isolation protocols and guidelines; (b) Isolation papers, as a record of practice under those protocols; (c) non-isolation cell-culture protocols, to test whether the dual-serum pattern also occurs outside Isolation; and (d) reports of practice under non-isolation protocols. Parts (a) and (b) are reported under R1 and R2; parts (c) and (d) under R4.

\subsection*{R1: Isolation protocols and practice}
We assembled peer-reviewed Isolation or primary diagnostic isolation methods that state both a numeric pre-inoculation (growth) serum percentage and a numeric post-inoculation (maintenance or virus-culture) serum percentage, including explicit 0\%. Rows were coded only when both values were explicit in the source. Every dual numeric pair was retained with equal weight, including the common 10\%$\rightarrow$2\% pattern and less common equal holds, increases, or near-serum-free maintenance. The resulting corpus comprises 170 papers.

Separately, we recorded published institutional recipes prescribing growth versus maintenance serum for diagnostic Isolation (ATCC; CLSI M41-A; ASM; NEADL/CIRAD PPRV SOP; WOAH Terrestrial Manual; WHO EPI; CDC measles laboratory tools; Health Canada/CCDR B95-a support). Eight guidelines with dual percentages form the plotted overlay.


\subsection*{R2: Isolation-stage negative controls}
The corpus was catalogued by defined criteria: each Isolation paper in the R1 dual-FBS corpus for which the full text could be read (158 papers) was assessed for the presence and quality of an Isolation-stage negative control on a four-tier scheme. Tier A: an uninoculated culture handled as the Isolation condition, including the matched maintenance serum. Tier B: an uninoculated Isolation-stage control whose medium match is not stated. Tier C: a control present only at another stage (titration, plaque assay, immunofluorescence). Tier D: no qualifying control reported. Papers whose full text could not be obtained were excluded rather than scored. Ambiguity was resolved to the lower tier.

\subsection*{R4: Non-isolation protocols and practice}
Cell-culture protocols outside virus Isolation (for example master-seed extraneous-agent testing, cell-bank qualification and routine maintenance) were searched for a prescribed change in serum between growth and later culture stages, recording stage, percentages and any stated rationale with the same coding rule as R1. The search started from USDA APHIS VIRPRO1013 (master-seed extraneous-agent testing). Reports of work carried out under these protocols were then searched to record the serum levels actually used. \pending{Search in progress.}

\subsection*{R3: Serum-only culture experiment and morphology labelling}
\emph{Culture experiment.} A U.S.-based contract research organisation (CRO) grew two uninoculated cultures from the same Vero E6 stock (ATCC CRL-1586), one in 10\% FBS (path A, Culture A) and one in 2\% FBS (path B, Culture B), for five days. The cultures were imaged by light microscopy and then by transmission electron microscopy. Full culture details and raw images are deposited on Zenodo (doi:10.5281/zenodo.17928456).

\emph{Descriptor set.} CPE descriptors were extracted from Table 7 of CLSI M41-A (Table~\ref{tab:clsi-m41-cpe}): rounded, ballooned/enlarged, syncytia, vacuolation, detachment, granularity, refractile, cytoplasmic strands, nonspecific degeneration, no CPE. Mapping the CRO image descriptions onto this set yielded the six-term CRO-comparable set used for per-frame comparison: dying cells (Dy), rounded (Ro), vacuolation (V), detached (D), granularity (G) and refractile (Re). Healthy-type terms (e.g. look healthy, well-defined nuclei, cytoplasmic extensions) were coded from the same descriptions.

\emph{CRO labels.} The CRO supplied written descriptions for groups of ten frames and for 22 individual frames (9 Culture A, 13 Culture B). Labels were coded from the report text by a fixed rule set; only a frame's own single-frame description contributes to per-frame analysis.

\emph{Automated labelling.} Computer-vision tools benchmarked against the 22 CRO-described frames did not reach accuracy adequate to label the remaining frames (Zenodo record 19607215), so human raters were used.

\emph{Blinded independent raters.} The image set comprised 99 light-microscopy images, 50 from path A and 49 from path B (days 1--5). Images were renamed with blinded identifiers and rated by two independent researchers with routine phase-contrast cell-culture experience (IR1, IR2). Raters were told the images would be used to train and evaluate AI vision systems and were not told the culture conditions, the absence of inoculum, or the hypothesis. The blinded raters processed all 99 images. Pass 1 was free-text description; Pass 2 was a ten-item structured checklist (three healthy items, seven CPE items) graded Yes/Partial/Mild/No. Pass-1 text was coded with fixed regular-expression lexicons; Pass-2 marks were scored as strict incidence and as a graded score (Yes 1.0, Mild 0.66, Partial 0.5, Minimal 0.25, No/minimal 0.15, No 0). Confluence was not used as an indicator of culture health.

\emph{Briefing-condition pilot arm (IR3v).} A third rater (IR3v) rated the same images under a briefing that differed from the IR1/IR2 brief: the IR3v rater was told that path B had been inoculated with a virus. IR3v Pass 1 text was scored with the unchanged shared lexicon and, separately, with a clarified coding that applies the rater's own answers to five clarification questions: shrinking was coded as the CPE class rounded/shrinking (Ro); the misspelling ``vaculation'' as vacuolation (V), while sentences describing a single large bright vacuole in about 1\% of cells, which the rater distinguished from vacuolation, were coded separately; refraction/refractive as refractile (Re). For projections the rater gave a two-part rule (thin and late in the time course = stressed; thick and early = healthy-type); no description stated projection thickness, so projections were left uncoded. \pending{IR3v Pass 2 checklist in progress.}
