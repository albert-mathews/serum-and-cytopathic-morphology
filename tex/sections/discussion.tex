\section*{Discussion}
\subsection*{Principal finding}
In uninoculated Vero E6 cultures, the serum step-down that published Isolation methods apply at inoculation was sufficient for the contract laboratory and for two raters blind to culture condition to describe more CPE-type morphology on the low-serum culture. Published Isolation papers rarely report the matched-medium uninoculated control that would separate a serum effect from an inoculum effect (11 of 173). Together, these results indicate that CPE read under dual-medium conditions is not, by itself, specific to the inoculum, and that the reporting standard for Isolation should include a matched-medium mock culture.

\subsection*{Genome-origin paths (P3)}
A documentary census of 109 reference-genome ``origin/confirmation'' deposit events found 86 of 109 (79\%) trace to culture-propagated sample production (39 with a stated CPE/plaque/syncytia warrant, 47 without), versus 12 (11\%) clinical-direct and 11 (10\%) other (eggs/animal). Because CPE under dual-medium Isolation is not inoculum-specific (above), sequence claims that use such Isolates as type material inherit that under-determination rather than receiving independent grounding. Consistent with this, the viral-genetics literature itself already records---often as technical noise---the predicted ambiguity: same-stock re-sequencing yields divergent consensus genomes (27 of 28 named-stock pairs surveyed), deposited ``complete'' reference genomes have been corrected or are single haplotypes of a mixed, passage-drifted population, and database-scale contamination has been quantified by the field. This is an existence finding (ambiguity is detectable, multi-form, across families), not a prevalence estimate; a rate would require a prospective re-sequencing batch rather than a literature retrospective. \todoA{Trim to the OB028 + OB150 claim spine on integration.}

\subsection*{Limitations}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Single cell line: Vero E6 (ATCC CRL-1586) only; results should not be generalised to other cell systems without replication.
\item Single laboratory execution of the wet experiment (CRO), with one culture per serum condition.
\item Limited human raters: CRO descriptions on 22 frames plus two blinded independent raters (IR1, IR2); feature-level agreement between raters is low even where direction agrees. A third rater arm (IR3v) is a briefing-condition pilot (not blind); both its passes are complete.
\item Automated vision tools did not label CPE reliably on this image set, so labelling depends on human description.
\item Briefing-condition arms are exploratory pilots, not blind replications.
\item The literature corpora record what papers report. A paper that does not report a negative control may still have run one, so the R2 analysis measures how often controls are reported, not how often they are run.
\item \assumed{FOIA results are not yet in hand; statements about reference-laboratory practice are placeholders.}
\end{enumerate}
