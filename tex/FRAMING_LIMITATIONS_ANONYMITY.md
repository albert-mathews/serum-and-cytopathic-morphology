# Framing, limitations, and anonymity (public)

Guidance for paper drafting and public repo text. Last updated: 2026-09-10.

## R3 morphology framing

From this small experiment, lowering FBS alone may suffice for trained readers to report more CPE-classified morphologies on the low-FBS arm. That questions the **specificity of CPE as an indicator of virus presence** under dual-media Isolation conditions.

Feature-identity agreement across raters is **not** required: directional excess of CPE-like marks under low FBS is enough to question the tool. This section describes Isolation-linked morphology scoring under dual-media conditions; claims are limited to what the cited experiment and corpus document.

## Participant anonymity (mandatory)

- Independent morphology raters and the contract laboratory (and their staff) remain **anonymous** in all **public** repo files, papers, Zenodo narratives, and talks.
- Identifying materials (legal names, invoices, named outreach) stay only in **gitignored** paths.
- Public text may say: "contract laboratory (CRO)", "independent researcher (IR1)", "blinded morphology rater".
- Participants were **not** told the research hypothesis. Public materials use role codes only.

## Experimental limitations (must appear in paper)

1. **Single cell line:** Vero E6 (ATCC CRL-1586) only; do not over-generalize without replication.
2. **Single laboratory execution** of the wet experiment (anonymous CRO).
3. **Limited human raters:** CRO image descriptions plus one independent blinded rater (IR1) so far; feature-level agreement is incomplete.
4. **Failed automated vision tools** for CPE labeling on this set (see Zenodo / methods).
5. Any priming / expectancy arms are exploratory briefing-condition pilots, not blind replications.

When drafting Limitations / Discussion, include (1)-(5) explicitly.
