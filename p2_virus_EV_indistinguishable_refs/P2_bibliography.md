## Fill rule

- A source moves into this bibliography once it is **fetched/read and contributes** (quoted, or coded
  useful with metrics in `P2_useful.csv`). Before the full text is read, an abstract-only ID stays in
  `P2_screened.csv` only (never here).
- Do not drop a row silently; mark `held_not_quoted` or move it to `P2_bibliography_unused.md`.
- Every row feeding the paper must carry a verified DOI/PMID (no guessed locators).
