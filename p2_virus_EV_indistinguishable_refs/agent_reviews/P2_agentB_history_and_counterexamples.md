## Timeline verdict (sentence by sentence if needed)

Synthesis §3 provisional timeline, evaluated against **screened** evidence (not hoped-for PDFs).

### Pre-1990s
> Classic **defective interfering (DI)** particles and herpes **L-particles** = noninfectious co-produced particles (queued full-text). Virus purification monographs likely discuss “cellular debris/membrane contamination” under non-EV names — **not yet systematically extracted**.

**Verdict: Correctly provisional; not overclaimed as established.**  
- P2-046/047 (DI class/Huang) and P2-055 (HSV L-particles) are logged **B**, not Y, with no full text and no quotes.  
- P2-048 (purification monographs class) is **B / not_found**.  
- Synthesis correctly marks these as queued rather than as demonstrated co-purification of *host EVs* with virions.  
- **Caveat for later drafting:** DI particles and HSV L-particles are historically framed as **virus-derived noninfectious products**, not as host extracellular vesicles. Equating them to the modern EV–virion dilemma is an interpretive bridge that screened evidence has **not** yet earned. Keep them as *particle continuum / noninfectious co-product analogs*, not as pre-1997 “EV contamination” primaries.

### 1997
> **Bess** and **Gluschankof**: microvesicles / cell membrane vesicles are major contaminants of **gradient-purified HIV-1**; same density range; EM heterogeneous. This is hard historical evidence that co-isolation predates the modern EV boom.

**Verdict: Substantively justified at abstract level; wording slightly strong on “hard historical evidence” pending PDF.**  
- Both are **Y**, primary_experimental, abstract_only (P2-010, P2-011).  
- Bess abstract (PMID 9126269): microvesicles co-purify with virions; band in sucrose at densities including retrovirus density; HLA-DR, β2-M, RNA/DNA; EM heterogeneous sizes/morphologies; p24/HLA ratios vary by isolate/host.  
- Gluschankof abstract (PMID 9126268) is **stronger than currently quoted**: uninfected-cell supernatants yield HLA-DR material banding at density overlapping infectious HIV; EM of infected-cell gradient preps shows **excess vesicles ~50–500 nm** vs **minor** ~100 nm virus population; budding co-localized with nonviral membrane vesiculation; shared antigens with exceptions (CD43/CD63 virus-biased; HLA-DQ vesicle-only).  
- Independent same-year *Virology* pair is real (not a single lab echo). Citation persistence in later purification literature is documented in secondary refs (e.g. Coren 2008 cites both).  
- **Not yet shown from screened corpus:** quantitative mass fractions, infectivity vs particle counts, generalizability beyond HIV/T-cell lines, or whether authors treated this as ontology crisis vs technical contamination of proteomics.  
- “Predates modern EV boom” is fair for **microvesicle co-banding language**; do not upgrade to “field knew EVs and virions were inseparable.”

### 2003–2006
> **Gould Trojan exosome** hypothesis; **Booth** shared budding domains; macrophage exosome-pathway budding papers. Dilemma reframed from “contamination” toward **shared biogenesis / continuum**.

**Verdict: Directionally right; evidence thinner than 1997 and 2016 clusters.**  
- P2-009 Gould: Y, abstract_only — continuum/ontology claim, not a separation methods paper.  
- P2-021 Booth 2006, P2-031 Nguyen 2003: Y, abstract_only — shared budding/release pathway support.  
- **Fair** as a literature *reframe* era; **not yet** quote-locked. Trojan is hypothesis/perspective, not proof of inseparability. Adversarial critiques (P2-065/066) correctly remain B/unresolved — synthesis §6 already flags this gap.

### 2008–2009
> **CD45 / subtilisin** methods (Cantin, Coren, Ott) = field admits problem by inventing remediation for proteomics of “pure” virions.

**Verdict: Core logic sound; bibliographic attribution partly wrong and must be fixed.**  
- **Coren et al. 2008 *Retrovirology*** (P2-014): CD45 immunoaffinity depletion of vesicles from Jurkat/etc.; explicitly cites Bess/Gluschankof contamination problem; claims CD45+ vesicles removable while virions remain. Abstract also *uses* CD45 presence on vesicles / absence on virions to **argue against** a shared Trojan budding pathway. So this paper is simultaneously: (a) problem admission + remediation, and (b) dual-classification reinforcement. Synthesis under-reports (b).  
- **Ott 2009 *Methods Mol Biol*** (P2-015): subtilisin digestion **or** CD45 depletion for biochemical studies — remediation protocols, not general proof of physical uniqueness of two classes. Subtilisin **shaves exteriors**; it is not particle-class separation.  
- **Cantin et al. 2008 is mis-catalogued:** real imprint is *J Immunol Methods* 2008;338:21–30 (PMID 18675270), **not** *J Virol* CD45 exclusive-capture as written in P2-013 / synthesis. Cantin’s actual claims (abstract): sucrose gradients inefficient; **Optiprep velocity gradients** separate AChE+ exosomes (8.4–12% iodixanol) from HIV (15.6%); **anti-AChE immunodepletion** also purifies. Nolte-’t Hoen full text already cites velocity/p24 vs AChE-CD45 logic.  
- **Missing foundational link:** Esser et al. 2001 *J Virol* (P2-070 is a thin B lead; PMID 11390619) — CD45 high on microvesicles, not detected on HIV-1 — underpins later CD45 depletion. Should be upgraded in Round 3.  
- **Logic that remediation implies a recognized problem is valid** and is among the strongest historical inferences in the corpus. Do not collapse “remediation exists” into “separation fails always.”

### 2010–2016
> Explosive EV–virus literature: EBV miRNA exosomes; HCV infectious exosomes; HAV eHAV; Nolte-’t Hoen **“almost impossible to distinguish/separate”**.

**Verdict: Best-supported era in the current corpus.**  
- Full-text seeds (P2-001, P2-005) plus abstract primaries (P2-016, 017, 018, 020, 044) justify multi-system expansion beyond HIV.  
- P2-001 “Mission (Almost) Impossible” section is the cleanest consensus-language anchor for incomplete physical separation (size/density overlap; density gradients not always reliable; continuum including defective particles).  
- **Bias note remains:** seeds overweight Dittmer/Raab-Traub/Nolte-’t Hoen/Margolis neighborhood; Round 2 partly corrected with Giannessi/Moulin/Dias but history still HIV-heavy.

### 2017–2020
> Method reviews (Raab-Traub/Dittmer; McNamara/Dittmer; Zhou RNA; Giannessi) codify multi-method co-purification tables.

**Verdict: Strong for methods-review codification; still review-heavy.**

### 2023–2025

**Verdict: Weakly supported as methods-dilemma continuity; better as “topic still active.”**

## 1997 cluster

### Are Bess/Gluschankof abstract-level “critical historical primaries”?

**Short answer:** They are **critical *candidates*** and already **usable as historical primaries for density co-banding / membrane-vesicle contamination of gradient HIV preps**. Calling them fully locked “critical historical primaries” for the whole P2 multi-metric dilemma is **slightly premature** without full text — but not wishful.

| Claim | Abstract support | Needs full text |
|-------|------------------|-----------------|
| Microvesicles/membrane vesicles contaminate gradient-purified HIV-1 | Strong (both) | Confirm methods, yields, controls |
| Density overlap with retroviruses | Strong (both) | Exact g/ml bands, gradient type, figures |
| EM morphological heterogeneity / vesicle excess | Strong (Gluschankof especially) | Image interpretation, counting |

### Mandatory full-text checks (Tier A — do not skip)

1. **Exact gradient conditions** (sucrose %, pad vs continuous, equilibrium density values for virus vs vesicle peaks).  
2. **Uninfected-cell controls** (Gluschankof abstract promises these — verify figure-level).  
3. **Quantitative ratios** (vesicle mass / HLA / p24 / particle counts; “major contaminant” as quantitative claim).  
4. **EM criteria** used to call particle “virus” vs “vesicle” (circularity: morphology used both to define and to count).  
5. **Infectivity / RT / genome** mapping onto fractions (are dense HLA+ bands noninfectious?).  
6. **Author framing:** technical nuisance for cellular-protein attribution in virion proteomics vs any broader particle-identity claim.  
7. **Cross-cite between Bess and Gluschankof** and subsequent remediation papers (Esser/Cantin/Coren/Ott chain).  
8. **Scope limits:** cell types, isolates, serum conditions — do not globalize from H9/PBMC HIV preps alone.

## Pre-1997 gaps

### Correctly queued (do not drop)
- **DI particle literature** (Huang/Baltimore line) — noninfectious co-products; density/velocity behavior historically used to separate defective from helper.  
- **HSV L-particles** (Szilágyi & Cunningham *J Gen Virol* classic; P2-055) — envelope/tegument, little/no DNA, co-produced; density distinct enough that field named “light” particles.  
- **Virus purification monographs / “cellular debris” / membrane fragments** (P2-048) — only path to pre-EV vocabulary.

### Overstatement risks
1. **DI ≠ host EV.** DI particles carry viral genomes/defective genomes; they support continuum of *infectious vs noninfectious virus-related particles*, not automatic identity with host exosomes.  
2. **L-particles ≠ modern EV purification problem.** They are a herpes-specific noninfectious particle class, often separable by density from H-particles — a **counterexample-ish** historical case of operational discrimination within “virus preps,” not pure inseparability.  
3. **“Cellular debris” language** may be dilute contamination talk without size/density overlap data; cherry-picking handbook asides would be wishful.  
4. Synthesis currently handles this well by saying **not systematically extracted**. Keep that discipline; Round 3 must produce quotes or demote.

### Under-logged pre-1997 / early leads to add
- Esser 2001 CD45 exclusion (bridges 1997 → 2008).  
- Trams et al. 1981 microvesicle ecto-enzyme exfoliation (cited by Coren chain).  
- Arthur et al. 1992 *Science* cellular proteins on HIV (pre-Bess host-protein problem).

## Devil’s advocate best case for separability

**Strongest published-style case consistent with screened evidence (and near-screen leads):**

1. **HIV is the hardest case, not the universal case.** Even there, multiple independent labs report operational discriminants:  
   - **Velocity (rate-zonal) iodixanol gradients** can resolve AChE+ / CD45-type EV markers from p24+ HIV peaks (Cantin 2008 abstract; echoed in Nolte-’t Hoen). Equilibrium sucrose density is the method that fails; velocity is not the same method.  
   - **CD45 immunoaffinity depletion** removes a major vesicle contaminant class from hematopoietic HIV preps while retaining virions (Esser exclusion → Coren/Ott protocols).  
   - **Subtilisin** strips vesicle-associated external proteome noise for internal capsid/pol analyses.

2. **Marker + function restore dualism for many experimental goals.** Field does not need perfect bulk physical uniqueness to claim two populations: infectivity, genome packaging stoichiometry, neutralization, capsid cores, and viral glycoproteins under cryo-ET can label subpopulations even when size/density overlap.

3. **Some viruses are easier.** Higher-density or larger viruses, non-enveloped naked virions vs membrane-cloaked forms, and systems with clean capsid lattices allow partial physical or immuno-separation that HIV+exosome literature problematizes.

4. **Remediation success is evidence of discriminability, not only of a problem.** Protocols that yield “vesicle-depleted virions” with altered proteomes are treated as valid by the purification community; if depletion did nothing, those protocols would not persist.

5. **Continuum language is compatible with operational classes.** Nolte-’t Hoen’s “border seems not to exist” is a perspective on intermediates; it coexists in the same paper with velocity/marker separation attempts. Ontology blur ≠ laboratory helplessness.

**Best single sentence for the adversarial brief:**  
> For HIV, equilibrium density and bulk UC do not uniquely define virions versus microvesicles, but velocity gradients plus selective markers (AChE/CD45) and infectivity assays are published as practical, if imperfect, separators — so “routinely inseparable by all means” is false; “not uniquely defined by classical density/UC alone” is true.
