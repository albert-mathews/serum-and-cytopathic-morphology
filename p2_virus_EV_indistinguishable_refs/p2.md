## P2 in one screen (mini-paper summary)

**Prediction (P2.v1).** Isolation's CPE endpoint is under-determined (OB020 + CRL041 + CRL042). If a second particle
class (virions) was never observationally grounded by that endpoint, then downstream purification / EM of culture
products should **not** uniquely re-separate that class from host extracellular vesicles (EVs) by physical parameters
alone. We predicted the EV–virus literature would *already contain* — framed variously as a technical contamination
problem, a biogenesis puzzle, or an acknowledged impasse — the ambiguity this predicts, and that the problem would be
**multi-metric** (size / density / sedimentation / precipitation / EM morphology), **multi-family**, and **historically
deep**. This is the hinge (CNJ050) applied to the particle layer (**CNJ052**).

**Methods.** A literature/organizational review (no wet work), run per `P2_research.md` v0.1: six search families
(separation-failure language; method-specific; cargo attribution; counterexample hunters; historical / pre-EV; standards),
two anti-bias rules (deliberately exit the seed neighborhood beyond Dittmer/Raab-Traub/Nolte-’t Hoen/Gould; abstain from
*abstract-only* claims — every load-bearing source read in full text), an explicit metrics taxonomy (M-SIZE / M-DENS /
M-UC / M-PEG / M-SEC / M-TFF / M-EM / M-LIPID / M-PROT / M-RNA / M-INF / M-MARK / M-CONT / M-HIST), and two independent
agent re-reads. Every screened source logged Y / B / N; strengthen *and* weaken quotes mined.

**Results.** 101 sources screened → **61 useful** → **42 quoted** (`[VERIFIED src]`, full text), spanning **1954 → 2026**
and rebalanced away from the retrovirus/HIV-heavy seed core (per AX012) toward influenza, herpesvirus, KSHV, rotavirus,
norovirus, reovirus, dengue (flavivirus), SARS-CoV-2, HAV/CVB3/PV/HEV, polyomavirus (JC/BK), and (as a boundary, not a
claim) retroviruses:

- **Bulk physical methods co-enrich, across families.** Density (M-DENS), ultracentrifugation (M-UC), polymer
  precipitation (M-PEG) and size-exclusion-type chromatography (M-SEC) co-isolate EVs with virions in HIV
  (Bess & Gluschankof 1997; Cantin 2008), **KSHV** (Chugh 2013), **influenza A** (Marichal-Gallardo 2017), and
  **dengue** (Reyes-Ruiz 2019). A 2023 review restates it bluntly: "there is **no universal methodology** to separate
  EVs from viruses." (Rey-Cadilhac 2023.)
- **The dilemma is historically deep, 1954 → present.** Pre-EV vocabulary documents the same overlap: **von Magnus
  1954** ("incomplete particles … practically undistinguishable from the fully active virus in size, shape and biologic
  surface characteristics"), Ada & Perry 1956, Morgan 1962, Spear & Roizman 1972 — then the 1997 HIV microvesicle
  primaries — the field's own 2016 "virtually impossible" (Nolte-’t Hoen) restated as "remains accurate today" (Nunez
  Lopez 2021) and again in 2026 (Defourny, Box 1).
- **Even non-enveloped "naked" viruses co-associate and spread cloaked.** eHAV (Feng 2013); enterovirus PS-vesicle
  clusters (Chen 2015); rotavirus/norovirus vesicle-cloaked clusters (Santiana 2018, 10–45% of stool pool, disproportionately
  infectious); rotavirus EVs (Iša 2020); **reovirus EVs (Smith 2024)**; **polyomavirus JC/BK EVs (Morris-Love & Atwood 2022;
  Helle 2020)** — the first *non-enveloped DNA* virus family; HAV/CVB3/PV/HEV enclosure (van der Grein 2018);
  multi-kingdom nonlytic membrane-cloaked egress (Altan-Bonnet & Panigrahi 2025).
- **M-EM is documentation-strong:** 1962 EM overlap (Morgan) + the 2020 "cup-shape is a preparation artifact"
  (Martins & Alves) + co-elution micrographs (P2-090 Fig 2) + naked-virus EV-enclosure EM (van der Grein 2018).

**Bounds (kept, not buried — the prediction is *scoped*, not absolute).** Separation **is** forceable under
marker- or method-conditional schemes: CD63 affinity (Chugh 2013), adapted Ficoll (Loret 2008), iodixanol velocity
(Cantin / Vaillancourt), CD45 / subtilisin (Esser→Trubey→Coren→Ott), heparin (Reiter 2019), nano-FCM with a fluorescent
envelope marker (Tang 2017), HCMV nFC (Bokun 2025). Each is **conditional** (assumes the dual classification, excludes
marker-negative particles, or uses a viral-encoded marker), and each was invented **because** co-purification is real —
none is a label-free proof of two particle classes (ADVR061). The 1954–1972 "incomplete virus / L-particle" primaries
are **virus-encoded defective particles**, not host EVs (the DI/L hedge).

**Conclusion.** **CNJ052 holds as a prediction whose target pattern is now extensively documented** — the field's own
words, across 72 years, in every major virus family, confirm that bulk purification/EM does not uniquely define a second
"virion" particle class separable from EVs by physical parameters alone, and that the EV literature's indistinguishability
is the *expected* pattern if that second class was never grounded at the endpoint. **This is a pattern-confirmation, not
a proof of "no infectious agent" (AX011), and not — by itself — the claim that virions were never observationally grounded.**
Co-purification ≠ no infectious agent; the ontology hinge (Layer 3) remains Discussion-only. The P2 result is a *bounds
and breadth* result: the dilemma is real, multi-metric, multi-family, and persistent — while being *forceable* only under
marked/conditional schemes that themselves concede the underlying co-purification.

**Adversarial balance (kept visible).** Counterexample-leaning sources are logged, not dropped: iodixanol velocity
(P2-072/077), adapted Ficoll (P2-088), CD45/subtilisin (P2-013/015/061/076), heparin (P2-080), nano-FCM with Env (P2-071),
HCMV nFC (Bokun 2025), and the 2024 reovirus neutralization shield that is *strain- and cell-type-specific* (P2-097) —
all bound the claim to "standard / untargeted bulk methods."

**Paper integration.** Default = one scoped **Discussion prediction** (P2-S1/P2-S2), pacing CNJ052 as "predicted,
pattern-confirmed," not as a settled elimination of a particle class. The ontology hinge stays a Discussion-only bridge
to the EV paper, not a result of this stream. CNJ052 already carries the corpus evidence in `CLAIMS.md`.

## What the corpus now shows (findings)

**Established — the dilemma is multi-metric, multi-family, and historically deep:**
- **Bulk physical methods co-enrich.** Density (M-DENS), ultracentrifugation (M-UC), polymer precipitation
  (M-PEG), and size-exclusion-type chromatography (M-SEC) co-isolate EVs with virions across HIV
  (Bess/Gluschankof 1997; Cantin 2008), **KSHV** (Chugh 2013), and **influenza A** (Marichal-Gallardo 2017:
  residual host DNA = "co-purification of extracellular vesicles … in a similar size range of the VP"); **dengue
  (flavivirus)** closes the family spread (Reyes-Ruiz 2019: DENV exosomes carry virus-like particles; viral
  particles "similar size to the exosomes … difficult to separate").
- **Historical reach is 1954→present, not 1997.** Pre-EV vocabulary documents the same overlap: **von Magnus
  1954** ("incomplete particles … practically undistinguishable from the fully active virus in size, shape
  and biologic surface characteristics"), Ada & Perry 1956, Morgan 1962, Spear & Roizman 1972, Szilagyi 1991,
  then the 1997 HIV microvesicle primaries — and the field's own 2016 "virtually impossible" restated as
  "remains accurate today" (Nunez Lopez 2021), re-stated again in Defourny 2026 (Box 1: viruses overlap EVs
  in size, sedimentation, precipitation, and buoyant density).
- **Non-enveloped "naked" viruses co-associate and spread cloaked.** eHAV (Feng 2013), enterovirus PS-vesicle
  clusters (Chen 2015), **rotavirus/norovirus vesicle-cloaked clusters** (Santiana 2018: 10–45% of stool pool,

  Altan-Bonnet & Panigrahi 2025 generalizes nonlytic membrane-cloaked egress across animal, plant, archaeal
  and bacterial viruses.
- **M-EM is documentation-strong.** 1962 EM overlap (Morgan) + the 2020 "cup-shape is a preparation artifact"
  (Martins & Alves) + co-elution micrographs reproduced in P2-090 (Fig 2) + EV-enclosure EM for HAV/CVB3/PV/HEV

### P2-S1 — the prediction, stated (Discussion particle layer)

> If the culture/CPE endpoint does not uniquely ground a second particle class, then physical overlap between
> extracellular vesicles and purported virions is the *expected* pattern. The EV literature records exactly that
> expectation: across 1954–2026 and many virus families, the field's own purification and electron-microscopy
> papers state that bulk physical methods — density gradients, ultracentrifugation, polymer precipitation,
> size-exclusion chromatography — co-enrich EVs with virions and do not uniquely separate them.

### P2-S2 — the scoped result

> This is a *breadth-and-bounds* finding, not a proof of physical identity and not a demonstration that nothing
> infectious exists (AX011). Separation is achievable under marker- or method-conditional schemes — CD63/CD45
> affinity, adapted Ficoll, iodixanol velocity, subtilisin, heparin, nano-flow cytometry with a fluorescent envelope
> marker — each of which assumes the dual classification or excludes marker-negative particles, and each invented
> *because* co-purification is real. The indistinguishability the EV literature reports is therefore the pattern a
> non-discriminating Isolation endpoint predicts, not a late technical nuisance.
