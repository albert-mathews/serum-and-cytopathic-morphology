## What the corpus now shows (findings)

**Established — the dilemma is multi-metric, multi-family, and historically deep:**
- **Bulk physical methods co-enrich.** Density (M-DENS), ultracentrifugation (M-UC), polymer precipitation
  (M-PEG), and size-exclusion-type chromatography (M-SEC) co-isolate EVs with virions across HIV
  (Bess/Gluschankof 1997; Cantin 2008), **KSHV** (Chugh 2013), and **influenza A** (Marichal-Gallardo 2017:
  residual host DNA = "co-purification of extracellular vesicles … in a similar size range of the VP"); **dengue
  (flavivirus)** closes the family spread (Reyes-Ruiz 2019: DENV exosomes carry virus-like particles; viral
  particles "similar size to the exosomes … difficult to separate").
- **Historical reach is 1954→present, not 1997.** Pre-EV vocabulary documents the same overlap: **von Magnus
  1954** ("incomplete particles … practically undistinguishable from the fully active virus in size, shape
  and biologic surface characteristics"), Ada & Perry 1956, Morgan 1962, Spear & Roizman 1972, Szilagyi 1991,
  then the 1997 HIV microvesicle primaries — and the field's own 2016 "virtually impossible" restated as
  "remains accurate today" (Nunez Lopez 2021), re-stated again in Defourny 2026 (Box 1: viruses overlap EVs
  in size, sedimentation, precipitation, and buoyant density).
- **Non-enveloped "naked" viruses co-associate and spread cloaked.** eHAV (Feng 2013), enterovirus PS-vesicle
  clusters (Chen 2015), **rotavirus/norovirus vesicle-cloaked clusters** (Santiana 2018: 10–45% of stool pool,
  disproportionately infectious), rotavirus EVs (Iša 2020), HAV/CVB3/PV/HEV EV enclosure (van der Grein 2018);
  Altan-Bonnet & Panigrahi 2025 generalizes nonlytic membrane-cloaked egress across animal, plant, archaeal
  and bacterial viruses.
- **M-EM is documentation-strong.** 1962 EM overlap (Morgan) + the 2020 "cup-shape is a preparation artifact"
  (Martins & Alves) + co-elution micrographs reproduced in P2-090 (Fig 2) + EV-enclosure EM for HAV/CVB3/PV/HEV
  (van der Grein 2018).

**Bounds kept (do not overclaim):**
- Separation *is* forceable under marker- or method-conditional schemes: CD63 affinity (Chugh 2013), adapted
  Ficoll (Loret 2008), iodixanol velocity (Cantin/Vaillancourt), CD45/subtilisin (Esser→Trubey→Coren→Ott),
  heparin (Reiter 2019), nano-FCM with fluorescent Env (Tang 2017; cf. ADVR061). None is a label-free physical
  uniqueness proof, and each was invented *because* co-purification is real.
- The 1954–1972 "incomplete virus / incomplete influenza / HSV L-particles" primaries are **virus-encoded
  defective particles**, not host EVs (the Agent-B DI/L hedge) — they show physical parameters alone don't mark
  the infectious class, without asserting virion–EV identity.
- **Ontology hinge stays Discussion-only.** The literature supports "bulk purification does not uniquely define
  a second particle class"; it does **not** by itself establish that virions were never observationally grounded
  (CNJ052 remains **predicted**, not established). Co-purification ≠ "no infectious agent" (AX011).

**Still thin / open:** none blocking. The flavivirus-EV gap is now closed (Reyes-Ruiz 2019 dengue, P2-096); the two
deferred leads are resolved (P2-094 Santiana, P2-095 van der Grein); the only unresolved item is the unlocated
"Gentry & Randall 1973" secondary citation noted in `P2_pdf_wishlist.md`, which needs no fetch.

## Stream status summary

The P2 prediction arm is **corpus-complete for its Discussion role**. The core claim — that published
purification/EM does **not** provide a method that uniquely separates EVs from "virions" by physical parameters
alone, and that the EV literature's indistinguishability reports are the *expected* pattern — now rests on 57
useful sources spanning seven decades (1954→2026), rebalanced from the HIV-heavy seed core toward influenza,
herpesvirus, KSHV, rotavirus, norovirus, dengue, and SARS-CoV-2 (AX012), with the dilemma documented by the
field itself at every era boundary (1954 → 1962 → 1972 → 1997 → 2016 → 2021 → 2026).
