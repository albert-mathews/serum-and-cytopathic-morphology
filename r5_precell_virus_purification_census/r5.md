  - **R5-B — warrant** `[VERIFIED src]`: "this is the virus" was a **transmission-and-correlation** assignment

    nucleoprotein, Bawden–Pirie–Bernal–Fankuchen 1936). The workers themselves recorded the limits: Smadel 1939
    ("impurities"), Knight 1944 ("ambiguous"), Sabin & Olitsky 1936 ("no unequivocal evidence").
  - **R5-C — transition motive (CNJ058)**: both strands present, **defect strand decisive** — necessary advancement
    amid recognized dissatisfaction, with a pragmatic cost/convenience incentive alongside, *not* replacement of
    quietly-adequate techniques.
- **Adversarial balance (kept visible).** **ADVR063** — the pre-1949 methods were *durable and gold-standard* (TMV →

## Snippets (paper-ready prose)

### R5-S1 — the scoped, factual version of the Introduction's "bottleneck" sentence

> Bulk purification of a virus — and the physical characterization that requires it — was, before cell
> culture, a capability confined to the agents that could be grown to high titer outside animal tissue:
> plant viruses such as tobacco mosaic virus (crystallized 1935) and bacteriophages. For the viruses of
> humans and animals, no general route of that kind existed; the first animal virus to be crystallized,
> poliovirus, was crystallized only in 1955 from tissue-culture-grown material. Cell culture therefore
> both supplied the material and, through the cytopathic-effect readout, credentialed the culture from
> which that material was drawn.

### R5-S2 — what a referee cannot rebut (process-critical, not "virology is false")

> These dates are a history of *methods capability*: absence of a pre-1949 bulk-purification route for
> animal viruses is a documented fact about the live-host bottleneck, not a claim that animal viruses were
> rare or unimportant before cell culture.

### R5-S3 — the pre-1949 warrant was correlative and transmission-based, not molecular

> Before cell culture, "this is the virus" was asserted by following a *biological activity* — infectivity on
> reinoculation, bacterial lysis, hemagglutination, or serological reactivity — through a purification train,
> and then by *correlating* a homogeneous, preferentially sedimenting, EM-visible component with that activity.
> The particle and the activity were equated by co-purification and proportionality, not by any direct
> demonstration that the observed particle caused the disease. That is a transmission-and-correlation inference,
> and it is corrigible — the founding example was corrected: Stanley's 1935 "protein" was shown by Bawden & Pirie
> to be a nucleoprotein, and the "crystals" to be liquid-crystalline.

### R5-S4 — the cell-culture shift lowered the bar from physical purification to a phenomenological read

> Pre-1949 bulk work *required* growing the agent to bulk and following a titer through ultra/gradient
> centrifugation. Cell culture displaced that machinery because CPE supplied a cheap, immediate
> "present-and-replicating" signal with no bulk growth and no purification train — so the operational product
> "Isolate" came to mean *a monolayer that degenerated after inoculation, passaged*, rather than *a purified,
> homogeneous particle preparation shown to carry the activity*. Whether that substitution was an advance or a
> retreat in evidentiary strictness is precisely what this paper examines.

## Research reference — the egg / hemagglutination warrant (recorded 2026-11-17)

An inoculated embryonated egg yielded **three** observables, none a direct sighting of the agent:

1. **Embryo death / illness.**
2. **Pock lesions** on the chorioallantoic membrane — countable; this is how Goodpasture's (1931) method was
   read for fowlpox/vaccinia.
3. **Hemagglutination (HA)** — the allantoic fluid gains the power to clump red blood cells.

**How HA became a "presence/titer" test.** (Hirst 1941; McClelland & Hare 1941, independently; it was
serendipitous, not designed — Hirst noticed RBCs from ruptured vessels clumping in inoculated fluid.) Its
"virus" meaning rests entirely on a chain of **correlations**, not a demonstration:

- uninoculated fluid does **not** agglutinate; only fluid from eggs inoculated with presumed-influenza material does;
- the agglutinating titer **rises over time** (the activity "multiplies");
- the activity **sediments and purifies in the same fraction as infectivity** (Stanley 1944), co-purifying with
  the ~100 nm EM-visible particle;
- antisera that neutralize infectivity also block agglutination.

HA is therefore a **convenient surrogate** whose "virus" reading is borrowed from the *infectivity*
(transmission) correlation. Agglutinating red cells is a generic physical phenomenon; and the HA/sedimentation
route did **not** cleanly separate "virus" from normal host material — Knight 1944 (J Exp Med; PMID 19871405)
showed a normal allantoic-fluid component co-purified and cross-reacted serologically with "the virus."
**Conclusion:** of the three egg observables, only reinoculation-infectivity carries even a transmission-style
warrant, and it too sits under the same under-determined endpoint. Death, pocks, and HA are all **derivative
surrogates** — a further **CNJ058** instance.
