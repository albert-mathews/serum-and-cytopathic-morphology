## 3. Why bulk is easy for plant/phage, hard for animal (working notes, to be grounded per item)

1. **Yield/titer** — TMV approaches a large fraction of infected-leaf protein (grams/plant); animal viruses are
   low-yield per host.
2. **Host scalability & cost** — plants and bacterial broth scale; animals/eggs do not.
3. **Stability/lability** — TMV (naked, rigid, stable) survives salt/acids; most animal viruses are enveloped
   and labile.
4. **Starting-material cleanliness** — leaf sap vs. animal tissue/fluids with abundant host protein/lipid/membrane.

## 4. Key boundary fact (anchor the Introduction/Discussion wording)

The first animal virus ever crystallized — poliovirus (MEF-1) — was crystallized in **1955** from
**cell-culture-grown** material (Schaffer & Schwerdt, *PNAS* 41:1020). This is the clean, citable demonstration
that animal-virus crystallization *followed* and *required* the cell-culture revolution. [VERIFIED src]
