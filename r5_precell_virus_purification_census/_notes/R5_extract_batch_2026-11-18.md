## R5-001 — Stanley 1935 (Science 81:644) · warrant: infectivity_transmission · [VERIFIED src]
> "A crystalline material, which has the properties of tobacco-mosaic virus, has been isolated from the juice of
> Turkish tobacco plants infected with this virus."
> "The infectivity, chemical composition and optical rotation of the crystalline protein were unchanged after
> 10 successive crystallizations."
- Stanley reported the material as protein (20% N, 1% ash; **"Molisch and Fehlings tests are negative"** — i.e.
  no carbohydrate/nucleic acid detected). That negative is exactly what Bawden & Pirie overturned (R5-016).

## R5-002 — Bawden & Pirie 1937 (Proc R Soc B 123:274–320) · warrant: liquid-crystalline nucleoprotein · [VERIFIED src]
- "Virus" preparation shown to be **liquid-crystalline**, not true crystal; infective at 10⁻⁸; contains phosphorus
  and carbohydrate (nucleoprotein), serological titre 1/4×10⁶.

## R5-004 — Kausche, Pfankuch & Ruska 1939 (Naturwissenschaften 27:292) · warrant: particle–activity correlation · [VERIFIED src]
- German; early EM of tobacco mosaic virus ("Aggregate des Tabakmosaikvirus"). Identity confirmed; note this is
  **not** the first virus EM (1938 poxviruses, R5-015, preceded it).

## R5-016 — Bawden, Pirie, Bernal & Fankuchen 1936 (Nature 138:1051) · the Stanley correction · [VERIFIED src] (OCR)
> "STANLEY has described the preparation of a crystalline protein… The crystals were small needles…"
- Reports **0.5% phosphorus and 2.5% carbohydrate — nucleic acid of the ribose type** — i.e. the "virus" is a
  nucleoprotein and the "crystal" is **liquid-crystalline**. The founding reversal (CNJ058).

## R5-017 — Hirst 1941 (Science 94:22–23) · hemagglutination · [VERIFIED src]
- Serendipitous: red cells from ruptured vessels inside the infected chick were observed to agglutinate in the
  allantoic fluid. HA became influenza's presence/titer surrogate (correlational warrant, not a demonstration).

## R5-018 — **Knight CA 1944** (J Exp Med; PMID 19871405) · contamination caution · [VERIFIED src]
> "The isolation and purification of animal viruses has been considerably complicated … by the apparent inability
> to separate cleanly the disease agent from cellular debris or to distinguish it chemically and physically from
> 'heavy' components of normal tissues. These difficulties … render chemical and physical analyses ambiguous."
- A normal **sedimentable component of allantoic fluid** co-purified with "the virus"; **author corrected from
  Friedewald → Knight**.

## R5-020 — Enders, Weller & Robbins Nobel Lecture 1954 · the "gate-switch" rationale · [VERIFIED src]
- Confirms Sabin & Olitsky 1936 context and states the key result as "the demonstration that poliomyelitis
  viruses multiply in vitro in human tissues of **non-nervous** origin"; contrasts monkey titration vs tissue
  culture. (For the cost/yield framing see the 1954 ceremony speech and secondary accounts.)
