- **HPV18-O1** (HPV18) — Cole & Danos 1987 J Mol Biol PMID 3039146 — `clinical_direct`, `[VERIFIED src]` (cloned from a cervical carcinoma of a Brazilian patient; X05015). Added 2026-11-18.
