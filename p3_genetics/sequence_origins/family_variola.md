- **VAR-O1** — Massung 1994 Virology PMID 8184534 · DOI 10.1006/viro.1994.1188 — `other`, `[VERIFIED src]` (OCR).
  Variola major Bangladesh-1975: "Virus was isolated from scab material ... grown on the chorioallantoic membrane (CAM)
  of 12-day-old fertile eggs, and a single pock was picked for passaging virus into chick embryo fibroblast (CEF)
  cells. Virus DNA was prepared from the second [CEF passage]."

Path class `other` (eggs/CAM + chick embryo fibroblast, "pock" on-egg readout — not monolayer CPE), parallel to influenza
PR8 (eggs) and YFV-17D. This is the "eradicated poxvirus" reference: its genome came from a stored scab isolate
propagated on eggs, not from CPE tissue culture.
