## 2. Working hypothesis (scoped)

Cell-culture CPE under dual-media Isolation is co-varied with enrichment and is not treated here as a virus-specific detector. The field nonetheless uses that process to assert Isolates, then characterizes products physically (P2) and often sequences them (or classifies clinical contigs by homology to culture-rooted references). EV research sequences particles from systems treated as non-viral.  

**Inquiry seed for the paper (not a result):** To what extent do public “viral” sequence claims and phylogenies reaffirm culture-system / reference-ontology labels rather than independently establish a discrete virion genome class? We do not resolve that here; we document the path and the epistemic gaps.

## 4. Agreed four-point hinge (feeds P3)

1. Culture-CPE Isolates seeded much of the **reference ontology** (type-strain audit: many high-path exemplars).  
2. Clinical mNGS often **classifies** novel contigs via that ontology (nucleotide/protein homology).  
3. Physical culture products overlap EVs (**P2**).  
4. Therefore apparent genetic distinctness of “virus vs EV” may be partly **label- and pipeline-dependent**, unless particle-matched, assumption-symmetric sequencing (± claimed inoculum, same purification) is shown — which is rare.

## 5. Scrutiny checklist for discovery / type-sequence papers

When reviewing Isolate→sequence or clinical-discovery papers under this angle, score warrants explicitly:

| Warrant | Strong if… | Weak / gap if… |
|---------|------------|----------------|
| Contig quality | Long contig, depth, coherent ORFs | Short leftover reads only |
| Host / blank controls | Host subtracted; reagent blanks negative | No blanks; kitome risk |
| “Viral” label | Independent of prior viral DB *or* multiple orthogonal lines | Homology to culture-era viral DBs as sole warrant |
| Causation | Not claimed from presence in sick hosts alone | “Found in patients” as etiology |
| Particle match | ± inoculum, same purification, same pipeline | No EV / uninoculated matched arm |
| Phylogeny | Treated as similarity to labeled refs | Treated as proof of a new particle class |

**Paper posture:** Present unambiguous gaps in the warrant stack (sow inquiry). Do not claim genetics has been overturned.
